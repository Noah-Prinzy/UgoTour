Using UgoTour

How do I explore a place?

Open Destinations, choose a destination or attraction, then UgoTour focuses it on the Map. Use the map callout to continue to details.

What is a planned trip?

Trips in UgoTour are personal travel plans stored in your account. They are not paid reservations with a hotel or tour company.

How do saved places work?

Use the heart controls to save destinations and attractions. Open Saved from the navigation to return to them later.

Why does the map need internet?

UgoTour can cache parts of the PWA, but live map tiles require a network connection.

How do I reset my password?

Use “Forgot password?” on Login. A time-limited reset link will be sent when production email delivery is configured.
