About UgoTour

Uganda, easier to discover.

UgoTour brings destination discovery, live geography and personal travel planning into one focused Progressive Web App built around Uganda.

01

Discover

Explore major destinations and local attractions across Uganda through photography, travel context and location-aware discovery.

02

Map

Move naturally from inspiration to geography with an interactive Uganda map, nearby discovery and focused place callouts.

03

Plan

Favorite places you love and keep planned visits connected to your UgoTour account for an easier return later.

UgoTour is a tourism discovery and trip-planning product. A planned visit is not a confirmed hotel, tour-operator or transport reservation unless a future partner integration explicitly says so.
