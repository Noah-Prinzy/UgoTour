About UgoTour

Uganda, easier to discover.

UgoTour is a JavaScript-first tourism Progressive Web App that brings destinations, attractions, maps and personal trip planning into one experience.

Discover

Explore major destinations and local attractions across Uganda, supported by photography, travel context and map coordinates.

Map

Move from discovery to geography through the interactive Uganda tourism map and location callouts.

Plan

Save places you love and keep planned visits attached to your UgoTour account.

UgoTour is a tourism discovery and trip-planning product. A planned visit is not a confirmed hotel, tour-operator or transport reservation unless a future partner integration explicitly says so.
