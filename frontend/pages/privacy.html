Privacy

Privacy notice

Pre-deployment template: review this notice with the requirements of the jurisdiction where UgoTour will operate before public launch.

Data UgoTour stores

Account name, email, password hash, optional profile image, saved places, planned trips, session records and messages you submit through Contact.

Why it is used

To authenticate your account, personalize your tourism experience, store your plans and saved places, provide support, and operate the service.

Security

Browser sessions use an HttpOnly cookie. Passwords are stored as backend password hashes rather than plain text.

Third-party map services

The interactive map loads map tiles from an external map tile provider, which may receive normal web-request information such as IP address and user agent.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, remove your profile photo, remove saved places, cancel trip plans and contact the operator about account data.

Contact

Use the UgoTour Contact page for privacy questions.
